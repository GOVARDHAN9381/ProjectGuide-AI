# Government Agricultural Schemes and Subsidies

## 1. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- Benefit: Direct income support of Rs. 6,000 per year paid in three equal installments of Rs. 2,000 directly into bank accounts of land-holding farmer families.
- Eligibility: All landholding farmers' families subject to operational exclusion criteria.

## 2. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)
- Benefit: Comprehensive crop insurance cover against yield losses due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, pest attack, disease).
- Farmer Premium Rates:
  - Kharif crops: 2.0% of sum insured
  - Rabi crops: 1.5% of sum insured
  - Annual Commercial/Horticultural crops: 5.0% of sum insured

## 3. Soil Health Card Scheme
- Benefit: Provides customized soil health cards showing status of 12 soil parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC) along with fertilizer recommendations tailored to farmers' fields.
- Frequency: Issued every 2 years to track soil fertility improvements.
