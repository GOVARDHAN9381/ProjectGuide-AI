# Government Agricultural Schemes and Crop Insurance Report

## Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) - Crop Insurance
- **Overview:** Comprehensive risk insurance covering crop losses from pre-sowing to post-harvest stages due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, flood, unseasonal rain, pests, diseases).
- **Farmer Premium Rates:**
  - Kharif Food & Oilseed Crops: **2.0%** of sum insured.
  - Rabi Food & Oilseed Crops: **1.5%** of sum insured.
  - Annual Commercial / Horticultural Crops: **5.0%** of sum insured.
- **Claim Reporting Timeline:**
  - Individual localized calamities (hailstorm, landslide, inundation, post-harvest cyclone damage) MUST be reported within **72 hours** of the event via the Crop Insurance App, Toll-Free Number (14447), or local Agriculture Department / Bank branch.

## PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Financial Support:** Rs. 6,000 per year provided in 3 equal installments of Rs. 2,000 directly into the Aadhaar-linked bank accounts of eligible farmer families.
- **Mandatory Requirements:**
  - e-KYC verification through Aadhaar OTP or Biometric at CSC centers.
  - Landholding details verified on State Revenue Portals.
  - Aadhaar seeding with bank account (NPCI status Active).

## Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme
- Provides farmers with field-specific soil test status across 12 parameters (N, P, K, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, Bo, pH, EC, OC).
- Recommends customized fertilizer dosages to prevent over-application and reduce farming input costs by 15–20%.

---

## Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM) & PMKSY
- **PMKSY (Per Drop More Crop):** Provides 45% to 55% subsidy on Drip and Sprinkler irrigation systems for small, marginal, and general category farmers.
- **SMAM Subsidies:** Provides 40% to 50% subsidy for individual farm machinery (tractors, power tillers, rotavators, sprayers, harvesters) and up to 80% subsidy for setting up Custom Hiring Centers (CHC).
