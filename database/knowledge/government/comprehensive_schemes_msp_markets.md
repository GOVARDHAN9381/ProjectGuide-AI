# Comprehensive Government Schemes, MSP & Post-Harvest Market Master Guide

## Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Complete Claim Workflow
1. **Scope of Insurance Coverage:**
   - Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk (up to 25% of Sum Insured).
   - Standing Crop Loss (Drought, Flood, Pests, Natural Fire, Lightning).
   - Post-Harvest Losses (up to 14 days after harvest for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition against unseasonal cyclone/rain).
   - Localized Calamities (Hailstorm, Landslide, Inundation, Cloudburst).
2. **Farmer Premium Share:**
   - **Kharif Crops:** 2.0% of Sum Insured.
   - **Rabi Crops:** 1.5% of Sum Insured.
   - **Commercial / Annual Horticultural Crops:** 5.0% of Sum Insured.
   - (Remaining premium subsidy is paid 50:50 by Central and State Governments).
3. **Mandatory 72-Hour Loss Intimation Protocol:**
   - For localized calamities, the farmer **MUST report the loss within 72 hours** of the occurrence through any of the following:
     - **Crop Insurance Portal / Mobile App (PMFBY app)**.
     - **National Toll-Free Helpline: 14447**.
     - Nearest Agriculture Officer / KVK / CSC Center / Insuring Bank Branch.
   - Required details: Policy Number / Bank Account, Loss Date & Cause, GPS Geo-tagged photos of damaged field.

## PM-KISAN & Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme
- **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi):**
  - Rs. 6,000/year in 3 equal four-monthly installments of Rs. 2,000 via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).
  - Prerequisites: Aadhaar e-KYC verified, Landholding authenticated on State Land Records Portal, Bank Account Aadhaar-seeded with NPCI active status.
- **Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Interest Subvention Scheme:**
  - Provides institutional crop loans up to **Rs. 3 Lakh at an effective interest rate of 4% per annum** (7% standard interest rate minus 3% prompt repayment incentive).
  - Also covers Animal Husbandry, Dairy, and Fisheries up to Rs. 2 Lakh.

## e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) & Minimum Support Price (MSP)
- **e-NAM Integration:** Pan-India electronic trading portal networking existing APMC mandis to facilitate unified online bidding, transparent price discovery, and direct payment into farmers' bank accounts.
- **Minimum Support Price (MSP) Determination:** Fixed based on the recommendation of Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) considering:
  - Cost $A2+FL$ (Actual paid-out expenses + Imputed value of family labor).
  - Guaranteed minimum return of **at least 50% profit over Cost $A2+FL$**.
  - Procurement carried out by FCI, NAFED, and State agencies during Kharif (Oct–Jan) and Rabi (April–June).

---

## Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF) & Cold Storage Subsidies
- Provides medium-to-long term debt financing for post-harvest management infrastructure (Cold stores, Dry warehouses, Primary processing units, Sorting/Grading lines).
- **Benefit:** 3% interest subvention per annum on loans up to Rs. 2 Crore for a maximum period of 7 years, with credit guarantee coverage under CGTMSE.
