# Soil Fertility and Balanced Fertilizer Management Report

## Balanced NPK Nutrition Principles
Optimal crop growth requires balanced macro and secondary nutrients in specific ratios:
- **Nitrogen (N):** Promotes rapid vegetative growth, deep green leaf color, and tillering/branching.
- **Phosphorus (P2O5):** Crucial for strong root establishment, early flowering, and grain formation.
- **Potassium (K2O):** Increases disease resistance, stem strength, drought tolerance, and fruit/grain quality.

---

## Crop-Wise Recommended Fertilizer Dosages (per Hectare)
1. **Paddy / Rice (Transplanted):**
   - Recommended Dose: **120:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O / ha**
   - *Application Schedule:*
     - Basal (At Transplanting): 50% N + 100% P + 50% K (e.g., DAP + MOP + partial Urea).
     - Active Tillering (21–25 DAT): 25% N top-dressed.
     - Panicle Initiation (40–45 DAT): 25% N + 50% K top-dressed.
2. **Wheat (Irrigated Timely Sown):**
   - Recommended Dose: **120:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O / ha**
   - *Application Schedule:*
     - Basal: 50% N + 100% P + 100% K.
     - First Irrigation (Crown Root Initiation - CRI @ 21 DAS): 25% N.
     - Second Irrigation (Late Jointing Stage @ 45 DAS): 25% N.
3. **Tomato:**
   - Recommended Dose: **150:100:100 kg N:P2O5:K2O / ha** + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate.
   - *Fertigation Schedule:* Apply N & K in split doses weekly through drip irrigation.

## Micronutrient Deficiency Diagnosis and Correction
- **Zinc (Zn) Deficiency (Khaira Disease in Rice / Bronzing in Maize):**
  - *Symptoms:* Dusty brown/rusty spots appearing on middle leaves; stunted growth and delayed maturity.
  - *Correction:* Soil application of Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 25 kg/ha or foliar spray of Chelated Zinc (EDTA Zn 12%) @ 1.0 g/L.
- **Boron (B) Deficiency (Cracking in Tomato, Poor Grain Setting in Wheat):**
  - *Symptoms:* Death of growing tips, hollow heart in potato, fruit cracking in tomato.
  - *Correction:* Foliar spray of Borax / Solubor @ 1.5 g/L during pre-flowering stage.
- **Iron (Fe) Deficiency (Interveinal Chlorosis in Calcareous Soils):**
  - *Symptoms:* Youngest top leaves turn pale yellow/white while veins remain green.
  - *Correction:* Foliar spray of Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4 19%) @ 5 g/L with 1 g Citric acid.

## Bio-Fertilizers and Organic Manures
- **Rhizobium:** Legume pulse inoculant; fixes 50–100 kg atmospheric Nitrogen/ha.
- **Azotobacter & Azospirillum:** Free-living / associative nitrogen fixers for cereals, millets, vegetables.
- **Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB):** Solubilizes fixed soil phosphorus; apply @ 5 kg/ha with FYM.
- **VAM (Mycorrhiza):** Extends root surface area, enhancing phosphorus and water uptake in dry conditions.
