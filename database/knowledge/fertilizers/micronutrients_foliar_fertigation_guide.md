# Advanced Micronutrients, Foliar Sprays and Fertigation Master Guide

## Critical Water-Soluble Fertilizers (WSF) for Drip & Foliar Application
1. **19:19:19 (Polyfeed / NPK Balanced):**
   - *Application Stage:* Vegetative stage (15–40 days after sowing/planting).
   - *Foliar Dose:* 5.0 g/L (500 g per 100 L water per acre).
   - *Benefit:* Rapid canopy development, tillering, and balanced root-shoot ratio.
2. **12:61:00 (Mono Ammonium Phosphate - MAP):**
   - *Application Stage:* Early establishment & pre-flowering stage.
   - *Foliar Dose:* 4.0–5.0 g/L.
   - *Benefit:* High phosphorus stimulates vigorous root initiation and profuse flower bud differentiation.
3. **00:52:34 (Mono Potassium Phosphate - MKP):**
   - *Application Stage:* Flower bud initiation to fruit/grain set stage.
   - *Foliar Dose:* 5.0–7.0 g/L.
   - *Benefit:* Prevents flower/fruit drop, enhances grain size, and increases disease resistance.
4. **00:00:50 (Potassium Sulphate - SOP with 17.5% Sulfur):**
   - *Application Stage:* Fruit enlargement / grain filling / maturity stage.
   - *Foliar Dose:* 5.0–8.0 g/L.
   - *Benefit:* Improves fruit color, sweetness (Brix/sugar), luster, grain weight, and post-harvest shelf life.
5. **13:00:45 (Potassium Nitrate):**
   - *Application Stage:* Fruit development under stress or high temperature conditions.
   - *Foliar Dose:* 5.0 g/L.

## Micronutrient Deficiencies, Symptoms and Corrective Sprays
- **1. Zinc (Zn) - Most Deficient Micronutrient in Indian Soils:**
  - *Symptom:* Bleached/white bands on maize leaves ("White Bud"); rusty brown spots and stunted growth in rice ("Khaira").
  - *Correction:* Soil apply Zinc Sulphate 21% @ 25 kg/ha basal, OR foliar spray **Chelated Zinc EDTA 12% @ 1.0 g/L**.
- **2. Boron (B) - Essential for Pollen Germination & Sugar Translocation:**
  - *Symptom:* Fruit cracking in tomato and pomegranate; poor seed set in sunflower and mustard; death of terminal buds.
  - *Correction:* Foliar spray **Solubor / Disodium Octaborate 20% @ 1.5 g/L** at pre-flowering stage.
- **3. Ferrous / Iron (Fe) - Interveinal Chlorosis:**
  - *Symptom:* Youngest leaves become pale white/yellow while veins remain green (frequent in calcareous and high pH soils).
  - *Correction:* Spray **Ferrous Sulphate 19% @ 5 g/L + 1 g Citric Acid** or **Iron EDTA 12% @ 1.0 g/L**.
- **4. Magnesium (Mg) - Central Atom of Chlorophyll Molecule:**
  - *Symptom:* Older lower leaves show purplish-red discoloration in cotton ("Reddening of Cotton") or yellowing between veins.
  - *Correction:* Foliar spray **Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) @ 10 g/L (1%)** at peak boll development.

## Soil pH Correction Protocols
- **Acidic Soils (pH < 6.0):**
  - *Amendment:* Apply **Agricultural Lime (CaCO3) or Dolomite @ 2–4 tonnes/ha** broadcasted 30 days before sowing during land preparation.
- **Alkaline / Sodic Soils (pH > 8.5 & High ESP):**
  - *Amendment:* Apply **Mineral Gypsum (CaSO4·2H2O) @ 2.5–5 tonnes/ha** based on Gypsum Requirement (GR) test, followed by heavy ponding and drainage (leaching of displaced sodium).
