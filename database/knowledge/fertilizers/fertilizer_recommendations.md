# Fertilizer Recommendations for Major Crops

## Rice Fertilizer Schedule (per Hectare)
- NPK Requirement: 120:60:60 kg/ha.
- Basal Application (at transplanting): Apply 50% N (130 kg Urea), 100% P (375 kg SSP or 130 kg DAP), 50% K (50 kg MOP).
- First Top Dressing (Active Tillering stage, 20-25 DAT): Apply 25% N (65 kg Urea).
- Second Top Dressing (Panicle Initiation stage, 40-45 DAT): Apply 25% N (65 kg Urea) and remaining 50% K (50 kg MOP).

## Tomato Fertilizer Schedule (per Hectare)
- NPK Requirement: 100:60:60 kg/ha.
- Basal Application: Apply FYM 25 tonnes/ha + 50% N + 100% P + 50% K.
- Top Dressing 1 (30 days after transplanting): Apply 25% N + 25% K.
- Top Dressing 2 (50 days after transplanting): Apply remaining 25% N + 25% K.
- Calcium Application: Foliar spray of Calcium Nitrate (0.5%) during fruit set stage.
