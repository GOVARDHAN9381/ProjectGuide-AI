# Rice Cultivation Guide

## Overview
Rice (Oryza sativa) is a staple food crop cultivated extensively across India during Kharif and Rabi seasons.

## Ideal Soil pH and Soil Conditions
- Ideal Soil pH: 5.5 to 6.5 (slightly acidic to neutral).
- Soil Type: Clayey loam or heavy clay soils with good water retention capacity are best suited for rice cultivation.
- Tolerant pH range: 5.0 to 7.5. Extremely acidic soils (<5.0) can cause iron toxicity, while alkaline soils (>7.5) can reduce zinc availability.

## Climate and Temperature
- Temperature: Ideal growing temperature is 21°C to 37°C.
- Rainfall: Requires 1000 mm to 1500 mm annual rainfall or adequate irrigation infrastructure.

## Water Management
- Nursery Stage: Keep soil moist but avoid deep submergence.
- Transplanting to Tillering: Maintain 2 to 5 cm standing water.
- Panicle Initiation to Flowering: Critical water requirement phase. Do not allow soil to dry out.
- Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD): Recommended for water saving while maintaining high grain yields.

## Nutrient Requirements
- Recommended NPK ratio: 120:60:60 kg/ha for high-yielding varieties.
- Apply 50% Nitrogen, 100% Phosphorus, and 50% Potassium as basal dose at transplanting.
- Apply remaining Nitrogen in two split doses at active tillering and panicle initiation stages.
