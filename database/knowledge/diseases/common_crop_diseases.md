# Common Crop Diseases and Symptom Identification

## General Symptom Guide for Farmers

### Yellowing Leaves (Chlorosis)
- Lower/Older Leaves Yellowing: Usually indicates mobile nutrient deficiency such as Nitrogen (N) or Magnesium (Mg), or early fungal infection at root level.
- Upper/Younger Leaves Yellowing: Usually indicates immobile nutrient deficiency such as Iron (Fe), Sulfur (S), or Zinc (Zn), or viral infection.
- Yellow Margins/Edges: Characteristic of Potassium (K) deficiency or bacterial leaf scorch.

### Spots and Blights
- Concentric Rings: Fungal infection (e.g., Alternaria early blight).
- Water-soaked Spots with Halo: Bacterial infection (e.g., Xanthomonas leaf spot).
- Powdery White Coating: Powdery mildew (Erysiphe spp.).

### Wilting and Stunting
- Sudden Wilting of Green Plant: Bacterial wilt or vascular Fusarium/Verticillium wilt. Check inside lower stem for brown vascular discoloration.
- Stunted Growth with Curling: Viral infections (transmitted by aphids, thrips, or whiteflies).
