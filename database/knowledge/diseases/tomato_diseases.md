# Tomato Plant Diseases and Remedies

## 1. Early Blight (Alternaria solani)
- Symptoms: Concentric dark rings (target spot appearance) on lower, older leaves. Affected leaves turn yellow around spots, dry up, and drop prematurely.
- Management: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23 SC @ 1 mL/L. Mulch around base and avoid overhead irrigation.

## 2. Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans)
- Symptoms: Large, dark water-soaked lesions on leaves and stems under cool, humid conditions. White cottony fungal growth appears on underside of leaves in morning.
- Management: Spray Metalaxyl + Mancozeb (Ridomil Gold) @ 2 g/L.

## 3. Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV)
- Transmission: Spread by Whiteflies (Bemisia tabaci).
- Symptoms: Severe upward curling, stunting, thickening, and yellowing of leaves. Reduced flowering and small distorted fruits.
- Management: Control whiteflies using yellow sticky traps and spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 mL/L or Neem Oil 10,000 ppm @ 3 mL/L.

## 4. Blossom End Rot (Non-infectious physiological disorder)
- Cause: Calcium deficiency coupled with irregular watering.
- Symptoms: Dark, sunken, leathery spot at the blossom end of the fruit (bottom of tomato).
- Remedy: Maintain steady soil moisture; apply Calcium Nitrate foliar spray @ 5 g/L.
