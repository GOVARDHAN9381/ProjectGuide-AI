# Rice Diseases and Yellowing Leaf Diagnosis

## Rice Leaf Yellowing and Symptoms
Yellowing of rice leaves can be caused by nutrient deficiency, disease infection, or environmental stress.

### 1. Nitrogen Deficiency Symptoms
- Cause: Lack of available soil Nitrogen.
- Symptoms: Older, lower leaves turn light green to uniform pale yellow starting from the leaf tips and moving backwards along the central vein. Stunted plant growth, thin tillers, and early senescence.
- Diagnosis: If yellowing starts on older leaves while upper young leaves remain green, it is Nitrogen deficiency.
- Remedy: Apply Urea at 25-30 kg per acre as a top dressing. Ensure soil moisture is adequate before application.

### 2. Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae)
- Symptoms: Diamond-shaped or spindle-shaped lesions on leaves with gray centers and dark reddish-brown borders. Large lesions cause entire leaf to turn brown or yellow and dry up.
- Management: Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 0.6 g/L or Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 1.5 mL/L.

### 3. Bacterial Leaf Blight (Xanthomonas oryzae)
- Symptoms: Water-soaked grayish-green stripes starting from leaf margins, turning yellow to straw-colored. Bacterial ooze drops may appear on young lesions in morning hours.
- Management: Spray Copper Oxychloride (2.5 g/L) + Streptocycline (0.1 g/L). Avoid excessive Nitrogen fertilization.

### 4. Iron Deficiency Chlorosis
- Cause: Alkaline soils (pH > 7.5) or dry upland condition.
- Symptoms: Interveinal chlorosis where upper young leaves turn yellow or white while veins remain green.
- Remedy: Foliar spray of 1% Ferrous Sulphate + 0.1% Citric Acid.
