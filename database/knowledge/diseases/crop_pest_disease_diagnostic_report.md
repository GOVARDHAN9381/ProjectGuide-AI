# Comprehensive Crop Pest and Disease Diagnostic Report

## Rice (Paddy) Major Diseases and Pest Controls
- **Rice Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae):**
  - *Symptoms:* Spindle/diamond-shaped lesions with grey center and brown margins on leaves and panicle neck (Neck Blast).
  - *Management:* Spray Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 mL/L. Avoid excess Nitrogen fertilizer.
- **Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB - Xanthomonas oryzae):**
  - *Symptoms:* Water-soaked lesions starting from leaf tip moving downward with wavy margins; leaves turn straw-yellow and wilt.
  - *Management:* Spray Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L + Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L. Drain excess water from fields.
- **Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas):**
  - *Symptoms:* "Dead heart" at vegetative stage, "White ear head" at panicle stage.
  - *Management:* Install Pheromone traps @ 5/acre. Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 4 kg/acre or Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 7.5 kg/acre.

## Wheat Major Diseases and Pest Controls
- **Yellow / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis):**
  - *Symptoms:* Bright yellow, powdery pustules arranged in linear stripes along the leaf veins.
  - *Management:* Spray Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt) @ 1 mL/L or Tebuconazole 25.9% EC @ 1 mL/L at the first appearance of pustules.
- **Karnal Bunt (Tilletia indica):**
  - *Symptoms:* Grains partially converted into black powdery foul-smelling spores (fishy odor due to trimethylamine).
  - *Management:* Seed treatment with Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS @ 2.5 g/kg seed. Avoid excess irrigation during flowering.

## Tomato Major Diseases and Pest Controls
- **Early Blight (Alternaria solani):**
  - *Symptoms:* Concentric dark brown target-board rings on older lower leaves, causing severe defoliation.
  - *Management:* Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 mL/L.
- **Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans):**
  - *Symptoms:* Water-soaked irregular greasy dark brown lesions on leaves and stems under cool, humid, foggy conditions.
  - *Management:* Spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil MZ) @ 2.5 g/L or Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L.
- **Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV):**
  - *Symptoms:* Severe upward and downward curling of leaves, leaf thickening, stunted plant growth, bushy appearance.
  - *Vector & Control:* Transmitted by Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*). Control vectors by installing Yellow Sticky Traps @ 15/acre and spraying Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 mL/L or Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1 g/L.

## Cotton Major Diseases and Pest Controls
- **Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella):**
  - *Symptoms:* Rosetted flowers, premature boll opening, damaged lint and seed kernel holes.
  - *Management:* Install Pheromone traps @ 5-8/acre. Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 1 mL/L.
- **Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV):**
  - *Symptoms:* Upward/downward leaf curling, thickening of veins, and small enation (leaf-like outgrowths) on leaf undersides.
  - *Management:* Grow resistant hybrids; control whitefly vector using Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.2 g/L or Neem Oil (10,000 ppm) @ 2 mL/L.
