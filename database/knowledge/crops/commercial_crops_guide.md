# Commercial, Cash and Oilseed Crops Comprehensive Agronomy Guide

## Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / G. arboreum) Cultivation Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Climate:** Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) or well-drained alluvial soils (pH 6.5–8.0). Requires hot, sunny weather (21°C–32°C) with minimum 180 frost-free days.
- **Sowing Window:** April–May in North India (irrigated); June–July in Central/South India (with monsoon onset).
- **Seed Rate & Spacing:** Bt cotton hybrids: 1.5–2.0 kg/ha. Spacing: 90 cm × 60 cm or 120 cm × 45 cm for high-density planting system (HDPS).
- **Fertilizer Schedule (per Hectare):**
  - **NPK Dose:** 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha.
  - *Basal:* 20% N + 100% P + 50% K + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate.
  - *Square Formation (35–40 DAS):* 40% N.
  - *Peak Flowering & Boll Development (60–75 DAS):* 40% N + 50% K + foliar spray of 1% Magnesium Sulphate + 0.5% Boron.
- **Critical Irrigation Stages:** Square formation, Flowering, Boll development. Avoid waterlogging during boll burst.

## Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum) Cultivation Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Climate:** Deep rich loamy soil with good drainage (pH 6.5–7.5). Tropical climate with high humidity (25°C–35°C).
- **Planting Seasons:** Autumn (Oct–Nov), Spring (Feb–March), Adsali (July–August in Maharashtra/Karnataka, 18-month duration).
- **Seed Setts & Spacing:** 35,000–40,000 three-budded setts/ha. Trench planting at 120 cm–150 cm row spacing (ideal for drip fertigation).
- **Fertilizer Schedule (per Hectare):**
  - **NPK Dose:** 250:115:115 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha.
  - *Basal:* 20% N + 100% P + 50% K + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate.
  - *Tillering (45 DAS):* 30% N.
  - *Formative / Grand Growth (90–120 DAS):* 50% N + 50% K. Complete all nitrogen before 120 days to ensure high sugar recovery.
- **Intercropping:** Short-duration pulses (Moong, Urad), Potato, Garlic, or Mustard can be grown between wide sugarcane rows.

## Soybean (Glycine max) Agronomy Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Climate:** Well-drained fertile clay-loam to black soil (pH 6.0–7.5). Warm, moist growing season (25°C–30°C).
- **Sowing Window:** June 20 to July 15 (after 75–100 mm monsoon rainfall).
- **Seed Rate & Treatment:** 65–75 kg/ha. Treat seeds with *Rhizobium japonicum* @ 5 g/kg seed + *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed.
- **Fertilizer Schedule:** 20:60:40:20 kg N:P2O5:K2O:S/ha applied entirely as basal. Sulfur is critical for high oil content.
- **Critical Stages for Moisture:** Flowering (R1-R2) and Pod filling (R5-R6).

## Groundnut / Peanut (Arachis hypogaea) Agronomy Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Climate:** Well-drained sandy loam or light red loam with good calcium content (pH 6.0–7.0).
- **Sowing Seasons:** Kharif (June–July) and Rabi/Summer (November–January in South/Coastal India).
- **Fertilizer Schedule:** 20:40:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha + **Gypsum @ 400 kg/ha** applied at peak flowering / pegging stage (40–45 DAS) for proper pod development and preventing "pops" (empty shells).

## Maize / Corn (Zea mays) Agronomy Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Sowing:** Well-drained alluvial or red loamy soil. Kharif (June–July), Rabi (October–November), Spring (Jan–Feb).
- **Seed Rate & Spacing:** 20 kg/ha for grain hybrids. Spacing: 60 cm × 20 cm.
- **Fertilizer Dose:** 120:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha (1/3 N + Full P & K as basal; 1/3 N at knee-high stage 30 DAS; 1/3 N at tasseling/silking stage 50 DAS).
