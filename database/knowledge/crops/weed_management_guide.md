# Comprehensive Weed Management and Herbicide Guide for Major Crops

## Paddy / Rice Weed Control Protocols
- **1. Pre-Emergence (0–3 Days After Transplanting / Sowing):**
  - **Pretilachlor 50% EC @ 1.0–1.5 L/ha** (or Pretilachlor 37% EW with safener @ 1.5 L/ha) applied in 2–3 cm standing water.
  - *Target Weeds:* Annual grasses (*Echinochloa crus-galli*, *E. colona*), sedges (*Cyperus difformis*), and broadleaf weeds.
- **2. Early Post-Emergence (15–20 Days After Transplanting):**
  - **Bispyribac-sodium 10% SC (Nominee Gold) @ 200–250 mL/ha** in 300–400 L water. Drain standing water 24 hours prior to spray and re-irrigate 48 hours after spray.
  - *Target Weeds:* Wide spectrum grasses, sedges, and broadleaf weeds.

## Wheat Weed Control Protocols
- **1. Phalaris minor (Canary Grass / Mandusi / Gulli Danda) Resistance Management:**
  - **Clodinafop-propargyl 15% WP @ 400 g/ha** OR **Sulfosulfuron 75% WG @ 33.3 g/ha** applied at 30–35 DAS (first irrigation stage).
  - For herbicide-resistant Phalaris minor: Apply **Pyroxasulfone 85% WG @ 150 g/ha** pre-emergence within 0–3 days of sowing, OR **Pinoxaden 5.1% EC @ 1.0 L/ha** post-emergence.
- **2. Broadleaf Weeds (*Chenopodium album* - Bathua, *Melilotus*, *Rumex*):**
  - **Metsulfuron-methyl 20% WP @ 20 g/ha** or **2,4-D Ethyl Ester 38% EC @ 1.0 L/ha** applied at 30–35 DAS.

---

## Soybean & Groundnut Weed Control Protocols
- **1. Pre-Emergence:**
  - **Pendimethalin 30% EC @ 3.3 L/ha** (or Pendimethalin 38.7% CS @ 1.75 L/ha) applied within 24–48 hours of sowing.
- **2. Post-Emergence (15–20 DAS):**
  - **Imazethapyr 10% SL (Pursuit) @ 1.0 L/ha** OR **Quizalofop-ethyl 5% EC @ 1.0 L/ha** for grassy weeds.

---

## Cotton & Maize Weed Control Protocols
- **Maize:** **Atrazine 50% WP @ 1.0–1.5 kg/ha** applied as pre-emergence within 2 days of sowing. Followed by **Tembotrione 34.4% SC (Laudis) @ 280 mL/ha** at 15–20 DAS.
- **Cotton:** **Pendimethalin 38.7% CS @ 1.75 L/ha** as pre-emergence. For post-emergence directed spray between rows: **Pyrithiobac-sodium 10% EC @ 1.0 L/ha + Quizalofop-p-ethyl 5% EC @ 1.0 L/ha**.
