# Seasonal Crop Planning and Planting Advisory Report

## Seasonal Crop Calendar Overview (India)
Indian agriculture is divided into three primary cropping seasons based on climate, rainfall, and temperature:
1. **Kharif Season (Monsoon):** Sowing in June–July, harvesting in September–October.
2. **Rabi Season (Winter):** Sowing in October–December, harvesting in February–April.
3. **Zaid Season (Summer):** Sowing in March–April, harvesting in May–June.

---

## What Crops to Plant in Kharif Season (Monsoon)
- **Primary Cereal Crops:**
  - **Paddy (Rice):** Ideal for high rainfall areas or irrigated lowlands with clay loam to clay soils.
  - **Maize (Corn):** Excellent in well-drained loamy soils across Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Bihar, Maharashtra.
  - **Millets (Bajra, Jowar, Ragi):** Highly drought-tolerant; best for rainfed, arid, and semi-arid regions.
- **Commercial & Cash Crops:**
  - **Cotton:** Suited for black cotton soils (Regur) in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Telangana.
  - **Soybean:** High protein oilseed; best in Central India (Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra).
  - **Groundnut (Peanut):** Thrives in light sandy loam soils with moderate rainfall.
- **Pulses:**
  - **Arhar / Tur (Pigeon Pea), Urad (Black Gram), Moong (Green Gram):** Restore soil nitrogen and provide high market returns.

---

## What Crops to Plant in Rabi Season (Winter)
- **Cereal & Grain Crops:**
  - **Wheat:** Major winter cereal across Punjab, Haryana, UP, MP, requiring cool growing season (10°C–25°C) and well-drained alluvial/loam soil.
  - **Barley & Oats:** Suitable for marginal soils with low water requirement.
- **Oilseeds:**
  - **Mustard / Rapeseed:** Sown early October to early November; thrives in light to heavy loam soils.
- **Pulses:**
  - **Chickpea (Gram / Chana):** Sown in October–November; deep root system makes it drought-hardy.
  - **Lentils (Masoor) & Field Peas:** Fast-growing nitrogen-fixing legumes.
- **Vegetables & Cash Crops:**
  - **Tomato, Potato, Onion, Cauliflower, Cabbage, Carrot, Garlic:** Require cool climate with moderate moisture.

---

## What Crops to Plant in Zaid Season (Summer)
- **Cucurbits & Vegetables:**
  - **Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Bottle Gourd, Bitter Gourd, Okra (Bhindi).**
- **Short-Duration Pulses:**
  - **Summer Moong (Green Gram) & Summer Urad:** Mature in 60–65 days between Rabi harvest and Kharif sowing.
- **Fodder Crops:**
  - **Sorghum (Chari), Maize, Cowpea:** Essential green fodder during peak summer.

---

## Soil-Based Crop Suitability Matrix
- **Clay / Heavy Soils:** Paddy (Rice), Wheat, Sugarcane, Cotton.
- **Sandy Loam / Light Soils:** Groundnut, Millets, Potato, Carrots, Cucurbits, Watermelon.
- **Black Cotton Soil:** Cotton, Soybean, Wheat, Sorghum, Chickpea.
- **Alluvial Loam:** Wheat, Rice, Mustard, Sugarcane, Vegetables, Maize.
- **Red & Laterite Soil:** Groundnut, Pulses, Millets, Cashew, Coffee, Tea.

---

## Contingency Planning for Delayed Monsoon
- If monsoon is delayed by 2–3 weeks: Switch from long-duration Paddy to short-duration hybrid varieties or Millets/Soybean.
- If monsoon is delayed by >4 weeks: Shift to intercropping Pulses (Pigeonpea + Greengram) or Sunflower and Fodder crops.
