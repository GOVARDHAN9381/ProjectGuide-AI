# High-Value Horticulture and Vegetable Crops Production Guide

## Chilli / Hot Pepper (Capsicum annuum) Production Guide
- **Ideal Soil & Climate:** Warm tropical climate with well-drained sandy loam or clay loam rich in organic matter (pH 6.5–7.5).
- **Sowing & Transplanting:** Nursery sowing in May–June (Kharif) or Oct–Nov (Rabi). 35–40 days old seedlings transplanted at 60 cm × 45 cm.
- **Fertilizer Schedule (per Hectare):**
  - **NPK Dose:** 150:75:75 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha.
  - *Basal:* 30% N + 100% P + 50% K + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate + 10 tonnes FYM.
  - *Top Dressing:* Apply remaining N in 3 equal splits at 30, 60, and 90 days after transplanting (DAT) with Potassium.
- **Major Problems:** Chilli Leaf Curl (Thrips & Mite complex) and Dieback/Anthracnose fruit rot.

## Onion (Allium cepa) Cultivation Guide
- **Seasons in India:** Kharif (sown June–July, harvest Oct–Nov), Late Kharif (sown Aug–Sept, harvest Jan–Feb), Rabi (sown Oct–Nov, harvest April–May; best for storage).
- **Seed Rate & Spacing:** 8–10 kg/ha. Transplant at 15 cm × 10 cm on flat or raised beds.
- **Fertilizer Schedule:** 100:50:80:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O:S/ha. Apply 50% N and full P, K, S as basal. Top-dress 25% N at 30 DAT and 25% N at 45 DAT. **Stop nitrogen after 60 DAT** to prevent thick neck and rotting.
- **Irrigation:** Light frequent irrigation; withhold water 15 days before harvest for proper bulb curing.

## Potato (Solanum tuberosum) Cultivation Guide
- **Soil & Temperature:** Cool climate crop (day 20°C–25°C, night 15°C–18°C for tuberization). Well-aerated sandy loam soil (pH 5.2–6.5).
- **Seed Tuber Rate & Planting:** 2.5–3.0 tonnes/ha of disease-free seed tubers (30–45 g weight with 2–3 eyes). Ridge and furrow planting at 60 cm × 20 cm.
- **Fertilizer Schedule:** 150:100:120 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha. Basal: 50% N + 100% P + 50% K. Top-dress 50% N + 50% K at earthing-up (30–35 DAS).
- **Earthing Up:** Essential at 30–35 DAS to prevent greening of tubers (Solanine accumulation).

## Brinjal / Eggplant (Solanum melongena) Cultivation Guide
- **Soil & Spacing:** Well-drained fertile loam soil. Spacing: 75 cm × 60 cm for bushy hybrids.
- **Fertilizer Schedule:** 100:50:50 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha. 50% N + Full P & K basal; remaining N split at 30 and 45 DAT.
- **Key Pest:** Shoot and Fruit Borer (*Leucinodes orbonalis*).

## Turmeric (Curcuma longa) & Ginger (Zingiber officinale) Production
- **Planting Window:** April to June with pre-monsoon showers.
- **Seed Rhizomes:** 2.0–2.5 tonnes/ha mother/finger rhizomes treated with Mancozeb @ 3 g/L.
- **Mulching:** Heavy green leaf mulching (15 tonnes/ha) is mandatory immediately after planting and at 45 & 90 days to conserve moisture and suppress weeds.
- **Fertilizer Dose:** 120:60:120 kg N:P2O5:K2O/ha + 25 kg Zinc Sulphate. Apply Potassium in 3 splits for high curcumin content.
