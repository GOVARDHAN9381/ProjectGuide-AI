# Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Guide

## Rice Pests and Management
### 1. Yellow Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas)
- Damage: Larvae bore into central shoot causing "dead hearts" in vegetative stage and "whiteheads" (empty panicles) in reproductive stage.
- Control: Install Pheromone traps @ 5 traps/acre. Apply Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 10 kg/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 0.4G @ 4 kg/acre.

### 2. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH - Nilaparvata lugens)
- Damage: Sucks sap from base of tillers causing "hopper burn" (circular patches of dried, burned-looking plants).
- Control: Avoid excessive Nitrogen fertilizer. Drain field for 3-4 days. Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG @ 120 g/acre or Triflumezopyrim 10 SC @ 94 mL/acre.

## Tomato Pests and Management
### 1. Tomato Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera)
- Damage: Caterpillar bores round holes into fruits, eating pulp and rendering fruit unmarketable.
- Control: Plant Marigold as trap crop (1 row per 14 rows of tomato). Install Helicoverpa pheromone traps @ 5/acre. Spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 1 kg/ha or Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 80 g/acre.

### 2. Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci)
- Damage: Sucks sap and vector for Tomato Leaf Curl Virus.
- Control: Install Yellow Sticky Traps @ 10-15 traps/acre. Spray Neem oil (10,000 ppm) @ 3 mL/L or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.5 mL/L.
