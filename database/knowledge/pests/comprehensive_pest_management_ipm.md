# Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Crop Protection Encyclopedia

## Sucking Pest Complex (Whitefly, Aphids, Thrips, Jassids, Mites)
- **1. Whitefly (Bemisia tabaci) - Attacks Cotton, Tomato, Chilli, Soybean:**
  - *Damage & Vectors:* Sucks plant sap, secretes honeydew (sooty mold fungus), transmits Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV) and Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV).
  - *ETL:* 6–8 adults/leaf.
  - *Organic / Biological Control:* Install **Yellow Sticky Traps @ 15–20/acre**; spray Neem Oil 10,000 ppm @ 2 mL/L or *Verticillium lecanii* @ 5 g/L.
  - *Chemical Control:* Spray **Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.2 g/L**, **Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 2 mL/L**, or **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1 mL/L**.
- **2. Chilli & Cotton Thrips (Scirtothrips dorsalis / Thrips tabaci):**
  - *Damage:* Upward leaf curling (boat-shaped leaves), silvering of leaf undersides, scarred fruits.
  - *Organic Control:* Install **Blue Sticky Traps @ 15/acre** (Thrips are specifically attracted to Blue color).
  - *Chemical Control:* Spray **Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 mL/L**, **Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 1 mL/L**, or **Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.3 g/L**.
- **3. Yellow Mites / Red Spider Mites (Polyphagotarsonemus latus / Tetranychus urticae):**
  - *Damage:* Downward curling of leaves (inverted cup shape), bronzing, thick brittle leaves.
  - *Acaricide Spray:* Spray **Propargite 57% EC @ 2 mL/L**, **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1 mL/L**, or **Fenazaquin 10% EC @ 2 mL/L**. Avoid synthetic pyrethroids as they flare up mite populations.

## Lepidopteran Borer and Worm Complex
- **1. Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) - Attacks Maize, Sorghum, Sugarcane:**
  - *Symptoms:* Ragged holes in leaves, abundant sawdust-like fecal matter inside the whorl, pinholes arranged in rows.
  - *ETL:* 5% damaged plants at seedling stage; 10% at whorl stage.
  - *Management:*
    - Seed treatment with Cyantraniliprole 19.8% + Thiamethoxam 19.8% FS @ 6 mL/kg seed.
    - Install Pheromone traps @ 5/acre for monitoring.
    - Spray **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen) @ 0.4 mL/L** or **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L** directed right into the whorl.
- **2. Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) in Cotton:**
  - *Symptoms:* "Rosetted" flowers with petals tied together with silken webbing; premature boll dropping; locule damage.
  - *Control:* Pheromone traps (Phero-Sens) @ 8/acre for mass trapping; spray **Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 1.0 mL/L** or **Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 mL/L** at 60–90 DAS.
- **3. Shoot and Fruit Borer (Leucinodes orbonalis) in Brinjal:**
  - *Damage:* Wilting of top growing shoots; boreholes on fruits plugged with excreta.
  - *Control:* Clip off and destroy withered shoots weekly; install Lucin-lure pheromone traps @ 10/acre; spray **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 mL/L** or **Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 0.3 mL/L**.
- **4. Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas) in Paddy / Rice:**
  - *Symptoms:* "Dead Heart" at vegetative stage (central shoot dries and pulls out easily); "White Earhead" at flowering (empty white panicles).
  - *Control:* Apply **Cartap Hydrochloride 4G @ 7.5 kg/acre** or **Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 4 kg/acre** in standing water at 25–30 DAT.

## Subterranean & Soil Pests
- **1. White Grub (Holotrichia consanguinea) & Termites:**
  - *Symptoms:* Sudden wilting and drying of plants in patches; root system completely devoured below soil level.
  - *Soil Application:* Apply **Fipronil 0.3% GR @ 10 kg/acre** or **Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 1.5 L/acre** along with irrigation water.
